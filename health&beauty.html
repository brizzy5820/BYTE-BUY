How do I choose the right skincare or beauty product for me?

When selecting health and beauty products, consider the following:

  • Skin Type: Dry, oily, combination, or sensitive.
  • Ingredients: Avoid harsh chemicals if you have sensitive skin; look for hydrating or natural extracts.
